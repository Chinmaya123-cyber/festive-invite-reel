# Style: Indian maximalism, Gen-Z cut

Split slides put pair A on the top half and pair B on the bottom; the halves slide in from opposite sides on the beat.

## Type
- Rozha One: desi high-contrast serif (NAMASTE, DIWALI). Yatra One: Devanagari.
- Anton: tall condensed (dates, numbers); stretch with `scaleY(1.55)` for a Humane look (ETHNIC).
- Shrikhand: funky italic display (YEH!, PARTY, THERE!). Bowlby One: chunky (NOVEMBER, ONWARDS).
- Playfair Display italic: notes and asides ("save the date!"). Bricolage Grotesque condensed 800: joker and address.
- Hard offset shadow in plum (`8px 10px 0 #2A0518`) for print depth; per-letter pop with a spring.

## Motion grammar
- **Stop-motion boil**: stickers re-sample at 8–12 fps with tiny random rotation/offset; text pops at 24 fps.
- **One hit per beat**: a slam, a card flip, a sticker pop, a font switch, a split-panel wipe.
- **Through-camera transitions**: the last "YEH!" and the joker card scale ×7 into the cut.
- **Camera shake** (16 px, 0.25 s) on section starts; animated grain + paper texture over everything.
- Moving vehicles: speed lines, dust puffs, a bob on the half beat, contact shadow.
- Hanging things (diyas, garlands) drop with a damped spring and sway from the top.
